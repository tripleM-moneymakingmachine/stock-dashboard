Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Risk

CEO search

Co-founder Jensen Huang resigned as CEO and left the board on Nov 12. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO and ran her first call on Nov 17; Altimeter urged the board to take its time, a process that could include bringing Huang back.

Tailwind

Vera Rubin ramp

Rubin is the fastest ramp in Nvidia's history, adding close to $20B of revenue in Q3. Q4 is guided to $130B, and management lifted its fiscal 2028 growth view to about 75% from 70%.

Watch

Memory costs hit margins

Gross margin held at 74.8% in Q3, above the 74% guide, but is guided to a 71–72% trough in Q4 before price increases restore 72–73% in FY28. Supply commitments rose to $390B from $279B, mostly multi-year memory purchases.

Watch

Receivables and inventory

Receivables reached $81.0B, or 64 days of sales, and inventory $39.7B. Free cash flow still rebounded to $43.8B from $21.4B as profit outran the working-capital build.

Risk

China and concentration

Nvidia shipped $2.4B of Hopper data center products to China in Q3, but the Q4 guide assumes no China data center compute revenue. Hyperscalers still bought $60.1B of the $107.6B data center total.

Calendar

Ongoing

CEO search

The board is looking inside and outside for a permanent CEO; Colette Kress is interim CEO and CFO.
