SK hynix Inc. · Nasdaq ADS (10 ADS = 1 Seoul share) · Icheon, South Korea

The HBM leader, now trading in New York.

SK hynix makes most of the high-bandwidth memory stacked next to NVIDIA's AI chips. Revenue rose 328% to a record ₩104.6 trillion (about $74B) last quarter at an 80% operating margin, beating consensus, and HBM4 should overtake HBM3E in Q4. The Nasdaq ADS listed in July in a record $26.5B offering and trades at a large premium to the Seoul shares, so the two prices are not interchangeable.

Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

HBM leadership

SK hynix is the lead HBM supplier to NVIDIA. HBM4 12-high is shipping on schedule, M15X will run at full capacity by year-end, and HBM4 should pass HBM3E in shipments and revenue in Q4. 2027 HBM negotiations are finished.

Tailwind

Contracted demand

Long-term agreements with 14 customers now cover more than half of 2027–28 capacity, priced within agreed ranges that track the market. Management expects demand to exceed supply through 2030.

Tailwind

Shareholder returns

About ₩100T is planned for 2026: ₩30T+ of dividends and ₩60T+ of buybacks and cancellations. The Q3 dividend is ₩20,000 a share (₩2,000 per ADS), half of it a special payout from the Kioxia gains.

Watch

ADS premium

The ADS trades roughly 40% above the Seoul shares after currency conversion (about ₩1.37M ÷ 10 ≈ $103 vs $145 on Oct 27). The premium topped 50% in July and could narrow.

Risk

Below-the-line losses

Net profit of ₩39.8T missed the ₩61.4T FactSet consensus: an ₩11.6T currency loss and a ₩22.9T investment-asset loss sat below the record operating line. Q2 had the reverse, a ₩63.3T Kioxia gain.

Risk

Cycle, capex and currency

SK hynix lost money for five straight quarters in 2022–23, and 2027 capex will rise significantly with Yongin Phase 1. The won strengthened from ₩1,549 to ₩1,353 per dollar in Q3, which lifts dollar figures but trims won revenue.
