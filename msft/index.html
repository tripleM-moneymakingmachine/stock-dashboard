Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

Azure accelerating

Azure grew 48% to $33.2B, beating the 44–45% guide, and is guided to 49–50% in the December quarter. Demand still exceeds supply.

Tailwind

Copilot

Microsoft 365 Copilot daily users rose about 50% from last quarter; paid seats, over 30M in July, were not updated.

Watch

Capex keeps growing

Capex including leases was $45.2B, below the $50B+ guide as some third-party capacity slipped. Q2 should top $50B and spending speeds up in the second half; about 75% goes to short-lived chips.

Risk

Backlog stalls

Commercial RPO was $677B, up 73% but flat with June's $678B, and much of it is OpenAI.
