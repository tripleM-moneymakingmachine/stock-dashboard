MP Materials Corp. · Class A · Las Vegas, Nevada

America's rare earth mine, backed by Washington.

MP owns Mountain Pass, the only scaled US rare earth mine, and is building magnet factories. A Department of War deal sets a $110/kg price floor for its NdPr and fully contracts its new 10X magnet plant. Q3 revenue of $148M beat estimates, helped by a one-off defense sale, and GM magnet sales start in Q4, but GAAP losses and heavy capex continue.

Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

Government backing

The Department of War price floor of $110/kg (worth $8.1M in Q3) and a fully contracted 10X magnet plant reduce commodity-price risk.

Tailwind

Magnet sales begin

GM qualification is complete, with saleable magnet deliveries and revenue starting in Q4. NdPr output topped 1,000 t for the first time.

Watch

Heavy capex

Capex was $420M in the first nine months against $500–600M guided for 2026. Free cash flow was −$125M in Q3 and cash fell to $1.34B.

Risk

One-off boost, still losing money

A $31.6M one-off SEG+ sale flattered Q3 revenue; the GAAP net loss was $20.7M and magnetics revenue fell 15% during the switch to magnets.
