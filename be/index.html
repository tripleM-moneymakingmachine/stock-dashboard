Bloom Energy Corporation · Class A · San Jose, California

Power for AI, delivered on site.

Bloom's solid oxide fuel cells let data centers make their own power instead of waiting years for a grid connection. Revenue hit a record $1.24B last quarter, up 138%, and 2026 guidance was raised a third time to $4.4–4.6B. Oracle, AEP and all major US hyperscalers are customers or have validated the product.

Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

AI power crunch

Oracle powered a Utah site in under two months and AEP is deploying 700 MW for Google. Backlog is growing faster than revenue, and capacity is nearing 5 GW a year with more room in Fremont.

Tailwind

Profitable growth

Non-GAAP operating income was $286M in Q3, more than all of 2025; full-year guidance is now $960M–1.03B with EPS of $3.10–3.40.

Watch

Working capital and margin

Free cash flow was −$181M in Q3 as unbilled contract assets doubled to $859M and capex tripled to $153M. Margin guidance stayed at ~34% despite the revenue raise, on ramp and expediting costs.
