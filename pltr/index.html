Palantir Technologies Inc. · Class A common stock · Aventura, Florida

Data and AI software for the Pentagon and corporate America, growing faster at $8B than at $2B.

Palantir sells software (Foundry, Gotham and its AI Platform, AIP) that lets governments and companies run decisions on their own data. Revenue grew 96% last quarter to $2.31B, US commercial sales passed US government sales for the first time, and the full-year guide was raised to about $8.53B. The catch: new signings rose only 6%, and at $178 the stock is valued at about 50 times this year's revenue guide.

Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

US commercial engine

US commercial revenue ($984M) passed US government ($953M) for the first time, and the full-year guide now calls for more than $3.559B, growth of at least 143%.

Tailwind

Cash machine

Q3 cash from operations was $1.20B, a 52% margin. Palantir holds $10.4B of cash and Treasuries and raised its adjusted free cash flow guide to $4.8–5.0B this year.

Watch

Signings slowdown

Total contract value rose only 6% to $2.92B (49% growth in Q2), and US commercial deal value grew 4% from June while revenue grew 29%. The CFO said new Sovereign AI deals ran shorter than usual.

Watch

International lag

The US is 84% of revenue. Sales outside the US, about $376M, grew about 26%, so growth depends heavily on one country's companies and agencies.

Risk

Valuation

At about $429B the stock trades near 50 times this year's revenue guide and about 126 times trailing GAAP earnings. Any slowdown would hit a price like that hard.

Risk

Stock pay and dilution

Stock-based compensation was $319M in Q3 (14% of revenue). It is the main gap between the 62% adjusted and 47% GAAP operating margin, and diluted shares (2.57B) run well above basic (2.41B).
