Lumentum Holdings Inc. · Common stock · San Jose, California

The lasers inside AI's optical wiring.

Lumentum makes the lasers, transceivers and optical circuit switches that link GPUs inside AI data centers. Revenue rose 137% to $1.26B last quarter and is guided to $1.6B. On Nov 2 it agreed to sell itself to Marvell for 4.5 Marvell shares each, and both stocks have slumped since.

Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

Co-packaged optics

A second major NPO/CPO win in Q1 is backed by more than $5B of purchase commitments, on top of the 1.6T transceiver ramp expected through 2027.

Tailwind

Optical circuit switches

Systems revenue more than tripled to $508M on OCS shipments. Management says OCS visibility has widened and it could become a multi-billion-dollar annual business.

Risk

Marvell deal

Holders get a fixed 4.5 Marvell shares each with no collar, so the stock now tracks Marvell. Shareholder votes and antitrust reviews remain; closing is expected in the first half of 2028.

Watch

Supply and cash

Supply, not demand, still limits growth. Capex rose to $230M in Q1 and free cash flow slipped to $38M; note conversions and RSU tax payments cut cash to $2.0B.

Watch

Dilution

About $1.3B of convertible notes remain, and shares outstanding rose to 90.7M plus 2.9M preferred. GAAP EPS of $3.54 absorbed an $89M stock-award charge.

Risk

Valuation and volatility

About 21× annualized Q2 guided earnings after the stock more than halved from its October high. Optical stocks swing hard on AI capex headlines, so position sizing matters.
