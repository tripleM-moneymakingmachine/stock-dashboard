Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

Helios customers

OpenAI, Meta, Anthropic, Microsoft and Oracle now run workloads on the first Helios racks. OpenAI and Meta have up-to-6 GW deals each, and HPE won a $12B Helios order from Vultr.

Tailwind

Server CPUs

Cloud and enterprise server sales each more than doubled, and data center operating margin rose to 34% from 25%.

Watch

Margins slipping

Q4 gross margin is guided to about 55%, below 56% in Q3 and the 55.3% analysts expected, as lower-margin MI450 racks ramp.

Watch

PCs and consoles

Gaming fell 40% in Q3 and AMD expects client and gaming revenue to drop more than 20% in Q4.

Risk

Warrants and cash

OpenAI and Meta hold warrants for 160M shares each; their fair value cost $1.1B in Q3 GAAP earnings and should rise. Inventory reached $11.5B and free cash flow fell to $554M.

Calendar

Helios volume ramp

Data center revenue guided up about 50% from Q3 as Helios and 'Venice' EPYC production scale.
