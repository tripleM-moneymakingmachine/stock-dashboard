SpaceX vs Tesla merger

SpaceX and Tesla have each set up special committees of independent directors to study a combination (Musk, Oct 20), but no terms are agreed and no deal has been filed; Tesla's 10-Q describes a broader mandate to deepen ties with SpaceX, with a merger one option. Barclays' and BofA's base case is SpaceX buying Tesla with its own stock. This page lines the two companies up on the same yardsticks to answer one question: if they combine, which side is the cheaper one, and which brings more future growth?

The verdict

Cheaper today (sales)Tesla
Cheaper on future earningsSpaceX
