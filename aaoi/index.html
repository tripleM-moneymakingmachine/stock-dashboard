Applied Optoelectronics, Inc. · Common stock · Sugar Land, Texas

Racing to build 800G and 1.6T capacity.

AOI makes optical transceivers and lasers, now mostly for AI data centers. Revenue rose 128% to a record $270M last quarter and it turned a small GAAP profit; Q4 is guided to $385–420M. It is still pouring cash into new capacity, paid for by selling shares, and its US plants are delayed by power hookups.

Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

Hyperscaler agreement

AOI signed a multi-year strategic agreement with a Tier 1 hyperscale customer, which management says improves long-term visibility. Data center sales rose 254% to $156M in Q3.

Watch

Capacity target cut

Capacity is near 330,000 units a month. The end-2026 target is now over 500,000 800G/1.6T units, down from over 650,000; over 1M is targeted by end-2027 as US plants wait for power.

Watch

Guide below consensus

The Q4 revenue midpoint of $402.5M trails the ~$427M consensus. Non-GAAP gross margin is guided to 29.5–31%, up from 28.7% in Q3.

Risk

Dilution and cash burn

Paid-in capital rose $1.53B in nine months as AOI sold shares to fund factories; the Q4 EPS guide assumes about 96M shares, up from 75M at end-2025.
