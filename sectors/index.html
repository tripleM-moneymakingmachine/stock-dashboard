Sector rotation map

Relative-rotation view against SPY. Across: relative strength (sector ÷ SPY, against its own 50-day average; above 100 = outperforming). Up: momentum of that ratio over 10 sessions. Sectors usually rotate clockwise: Improving → Leading → Weakening → Lagging. Ratios use a 10-day EMA of sector ÷ SPY. Tails are weekly points; the dot is today.

Each card pairs the model's read (what the numbers say) with the research view (what is driving them and what to do). Stances are relative to the sector's S&P 500 weight.

Prices: Yahoo Finance daily bars for the Select Sector SPDR ETFs, SPY and RSP (total return uses dividend-adjusted closes), refreshed with every dashboard deploy. Flows: State Street Global Advisors daily NAV history (change in shares outstanding × NAV), refreshed daily. Sector weights: Schwab Center for Financial Research (as of Oct 2, 2026), rolled forward by price. Valuation and earnings: FactSet Earnings Insight and Siblis Research. ETFs track the Select Sector indices, which cap single stocks and can differ slightly from the S&P 500 GICS sectors. Commentary is a research view for information only, not investment advice.
