Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

The ad engine

Revenue grew 26% in Q3 as ad impressions rose 10% and price per ad 14%. The Q4 guide of $73–76B implies about 22–27% growth, and Meta expects strong growth through 2027.

Tailwind

Muse and leaner costs

More than 10M people set up the Muse AI agent in about six weeks. Headcount is down 15% YoY to 66,775, and Reality Labs losses should narrow to about $15B in 2027.

Watch

Capex and cash flow

2026 capex is guided at $135–145B, implying about $49–59B in Q4. Free cash flow was −$2.1B in Q3 on Meta's measure, and buybacks have stopped.

Watch

Borrowing and the Class C share

Long-term debt rose to $80.9B from $58.7B in December, about equal to cash and securities of $82.4B. A non-voting Class C share (one per existing share, due Jan 2027) can be used to raise capital or pay for deals.

Risk

Legal bills

The attorneys-general deal provides up to about $18.6B in payments, and a New Mexico judge ordered $25B over Cambridge Analytica, which Meta will appeal. More youth-safety trials are scheduled.

Risk

Reality Labs

Lost $4.13B in Q3 and is guided to lose $18–19B in 2026, on revenue of under $0.5B a quarter.

Calendar

Late Jan 2027

Q4 and FY26 results

Guide: revenue $73–76B. Expected to include 2027 capex and expense outlooks.

Jan 19, 2027

Class C shares

Intended effective date of the non-voting Class C stock; one share is paid per Class A or B share and the Class A price should adjust like a 2-for-1 split.

2026–2027

Youth-safety trials and appeals

More US trials are scheduled, and Meta is appealing the $25B New Mexico ruling.
