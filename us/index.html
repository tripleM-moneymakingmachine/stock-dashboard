US

The CME Three-Month SOFR (SR3) futures strip: each contract settles at 100 minus SOFR compounded over its three-month reference quarter, so the strip is the market's quarter-by-quarter path for US overnight funding rates and, through it, for Fed policy. Prices are delayed and refresh through the day; changes are shown in basis points of implied rate (a rate rise is a price fall).

FedWatch-style method on CBOT ZQ settles, shared with the Macro tab. P(hike) is the chance of a range above today's after that meeting. The SOFR strip and ZQ path agree within a few basis points; gaps beyond that usually reflect repo funding pressure (SOFR above EFFR) or term premium in the deferred contracts.

Futures: CME Group Three-Month SOFR futures (SR3), quarterly contracts, delayed quotes via Yahoo Finance, refreshed hourly; a settle for each session is recorded after the 17:00 New York close.
