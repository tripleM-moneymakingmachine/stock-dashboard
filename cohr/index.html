Coherent Corp. · Common stock · Saxonburg, Pennsylvania

AI optics growth, paid for up front.

Coherent makes optical transceivers, indium phosphide lasers and industrial lasers. Datacenter & Communications is now 82% of revenue and grew 79% last quarter, and management expects FY27 growth above 60% and a $3B quarter by June 2027. Paying for that growth took $776M of capex in one quarter, so free cash flow is deeply negative.

Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

Data center demand

Datacenter & Communications grew 79% YoY to $1.95B in Q1, and the Q2 guide of $2.55–2.75B was about 4% above consensus. Management sees a $3B quarter by June 2027 and a $4B quarter in FY28.

Tailwind

Margins rising

Non-GAAP gross margin rose to 40.6% on pricing and cost cuts, and the CFO raised the long-term goal: a quarter above 45% before the end of 2028.

Tailwind

Indium phosphide scale-up

6-inch InP lines run in Texas and Sweden, with Zurich due in the first half of 2027. A 6-inch wafer gives four times the output of a 3-inch one at half the cost.

Watch

Cash burn from capex

Q1 capex jumped to $776M while operating cash flow was −$93M, so free cash flow was about −$868M. Cash fell to $1.55B against $4.0B of debt.

Risk

Industrial weakness

Industrial revenue fell 15% YoY to $419M as Coherent leans into AI.

Risk

Execution and sentiment

InP capacity is the bottleneck; any slip in yields or the ramp caps revenue. The stock is more than half below its June high as AI hardware sold off, so position sizing matters.
