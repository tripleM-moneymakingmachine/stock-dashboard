Berkshire Hathaway Inc. · Class A · Omaha, Nebraska

Buffett's conglomerate, now Abel's to run.

Berkshire owns GEICO and other insurers, the BNSF railroad, Berkshire Hathaway Energy and dozens of manufacturing and retail businesses, plus a stock portfolio led by Apple, American Express and Alphabet. Greg Abel became CEO in January 2026. Q3 revenue rose 11% to $105.6B, but operating earnings fell 6% to $12.7B as insurance underwriting dropped 43%. Berkshire bought back a record $9.6B of stock in Q3 and holds $377B of cash and Treasury bills.

Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

Abel is putting cash to work

Buybacks rose every quarter of 2026 ($235M, $4.5B, $9.6B), and Berkshire bought OxyChem ($9.4B), Taylor Morrison ($6.8B) and a large Alphabet stake.

Tailwind

Railroad, energy and industry growing

In Q3 BNSF earnings rose 19% to $1.73B, BHE 8% to $1.61B, and manufacturing, service and retailing 12% to $4.04B. Nine-month operating earnings are up 8% to $37.0B.

Watch

$377B cash pile, falling yields

Cash and T-bills rose to $377.4B ($357B net of unsettled T-bill buys). Insurance investment income fell 14% to $2.73B in Q3 as short-term rates fell.

Risk

GEICO and wildfire exposure

GEICO's loss ratio rose to 76.6% from 71.5% as it spent more on ads and claims grew. PacifiCorp has booked $2.85B of cumulative probable wildfire losses, with the James appeal still before Oregon's Supreme Court.
