Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

Datacenter as a growth pillar

Datacenter revenue rose 61% in a quarter to $4.80B, 44% of sales, up from $269M a year earlier. Management says the long-term-deal pipeline exceeds its supply through the end of the decade.

Tailwind

Pricing power

Q1 growth was about 60% volume and 40% price, a healthier mix than Q4. Q2 FY27 is guided to another record, $12.2–12.6B at 84.5–85.5% gross margin, with modest price increases.

Watch

Cash returns

No debt and $5.1B of cash. Sandisk bought back $5.0B of stock (3.2M pre-split shares, about 25.6M post-split) in Q1, leaving about $10.5B of the $15.5B authorization. The 8-for-1 split took effect Nov 20 and does not change the company's value.

Watch

Customer prepayments

Eight NBM customers have committed to $93.9B of minimum revenue, backed by $16.5B of deposits and guarantees, covering about half of FY27 bits and two-thirds of FY28. Q1 free cash flow was $6.3B reported and $5.7B adjusted.

Risk

NAND is cyclical

Margins went from 26% to 85% in a year on price. When supply catches up, prices and margins can fall just as fast. Consumer revenue fell 29% last quarter and 39% from a year earlier.

Risk

Volatility

Beta is above 5. The stock fell 56% in five weeks after the June peak and dropped 5.3% on report day itself; after hours it swung between about −3% and +3%.
